Our History

REMAPRED was born with a clear purpose: to equip professionals in the Peruvian industry with the best thermal imaging technology available globally. As specialized distributors, we are proud to offer solutions that save time, prevent critical failures, and improve operational safety.

We are not just sellers; we are technical advisors. We deeply understand the FOTRIC portfolio and know that every application (from electrical inspection to building maintenance) requires the right camera. That is why we dedicate ourselves to listening, advising, and delivering excellence promptly and safely nationwide.

Our Mission

To provide maintenance, construction, and research professionals in Peru with world-class FOTRIC thermal imaging technology, offering transparent advice, agile deliveries, and unmatched technical backing.

Our Vision

To consolidate ourselves as the main and most reliable specialized distributor of thermal cameras nationwide, recognized for our deep product knowledge and customer service.
